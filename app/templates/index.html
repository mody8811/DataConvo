> User: "Which product categories generated the most revenue last quarter?"
→ Understanding intent: "product categories", "revenue", "last quarter"
→ Finding relevant data: orders, order_items, products
→ Connecting tables: orders → order_items → products
SELECT p.category, SUM(oi.revenue) AS revenue FROM orders o JOIN order_items oi ON o.id = oi.order_id JOIN products p ON oi.product_id = p.id WHERE DATE_TRUNC('quarter', o.created_at) = DATE_TRUNC('quarter', NOW() - INTERVAL '1 quarter') GROUP BY p.category ORDER BY revenue DESC;
→ Generating SQL: JOIN on order_id and product_id, quarterly filter
→ Answer: Electronics $1.2M · Apparel $890K · Home & Garden $742K

⚠️ THE RAG ILLUSION

RAG is Built for PDFs. Not Relational Databases.

Vector embeddings rely on fuzzy matching and struggle with aggregations, real-time syncs, and multi-hop joins. Stop trying to force document-search tech onto structured data.

🧮

Fuzzy Math vs. Database Engine Execution

RAG forces LLMs to do math on retrieved text chunks (causing hallucinated numbers). Data Convo generates exact SQL, letting your database engine compute 100% accurate aggregations.

⏱️

Stale Vector Syncs vs. Live Real-Time Queries

Vector DBs require constant, expensive data pipelines. Data Convo queries your live schema directly — meaning your insights are never out of date.

📦

Token Window Stuffing vs. Infinite Scale

RAG crashes when trying to stuff thousands of database rows into an LLM context window. We only pass schema metadata, allowing you to query billions of rows safely.

🔗

Relational Blindness vs. Hard FK Constraints

Embeddings have zero concept of foreign keys. Our engine respects physical FK constraints so multi-hop joins never break.
